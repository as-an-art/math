math as an art

This project aims to envision a future for human-centered math organized under the umbrella of what might be called the mathematical arts: mathematical pursuits centered in ideals such as “creative expression, storytelling, and cultural participation”,en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_arts rather than technological progress.

If this project something that resonates with you and you’re interested in contributing to, please feel free to reach out! I am best contacted by email: tyler.chen@nyu.edu!
