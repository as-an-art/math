title: math as an art

This project aims to envision a future for human-centered math organized under the umbrella of what might be called the *mathematical arts*: mathematical pursuits centered in ideals such as “creative expression, storytelling, and cultural participation”,[^the-arts] rather than technological progress.

[^the-arts]: [`en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_arts`](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_arts)

If this project something that resonates with you and you're interested in contributing to, please feel free to reach out! I am best contacted by email: [`tyler.chen@nyu.edu`](mailto:tyler.chen@nyu.edu)!
